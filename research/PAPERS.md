## Paper 1

WHO Recommendations for the Prevention and Treatment of Postpartum Haemorrhage

Completed

Main Takeaways

- Oxytocin is the preferred uterotonic.
- Tranexamic acid should be used early.
- Rapid recognition of postpartum haemorrhage is essential.
- Early intervention reduces maternal deaths.

Ideas for Project Asteria

- Detect warning signs early.
- Screen for postpartum haemorrhage risk.
- AI-assisted maternal risk prediction.
