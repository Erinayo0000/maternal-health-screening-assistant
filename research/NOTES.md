# Resource 4

## Title

Invisible Women - Chapter 4

**Author:** Caroline Criado Perez

**Date Read:** 23/7/26

**Type:**
Book

---

### Summary

The chapter explores how unpaid care work, invisible labour, and the gender data gap affect women's lives. It demonstrates that many policies fail because they are based on incomplete data that often excludes women's experiences.

---

### Key Findings

- Women perform a large proportion of unpaid care work.
- Much of women's work is invisible in official statistics.
- Poor data leads to poor policy decisions.

---

### Questions I Have

- How can digital health tools collect better maternal data?
- What important maternal information is still missing from healthcare systems?

---

### How This Helps Project Asteria

- Build data collection that reflects women's real healthcare needs.
- Ensure important maternal information is not overlooked.

---

### Ideas Generated

- Collect more detailed maternal information.
- Build future AI models using representative maternal data.

**Author:** Caroline Criado Perez

**Date Read:** 24/7/26

**Type:**
Book

---

### Summary

This chapter discusses how women have historically been underrepresented in medical research. Male bodies have frequently been treated as the default, leading to treatments and guidelines that may not adequately address women's healthcare needs.

---

### Key Findings

- Women have historically been excluded from many medical studies.
- Drug testing has often focused on men.
- Medical guidelines based on male data may not work equally well for women.

---

### Questions I Have

- How can AI reduce gender bias instead of reinforcing it?
- What maternal datasets are available for AI research?

---

### How This Helps Project Asteria

- Ensure future AI models are trained using representative maternal health data.
- Build explainable AI that healthcare workers can trust.

---

### Ideas Generated

- Explainable AI recommendations.

# Resource 7

## Title

WHO Recommendations for the Prevention and Treatment of Postpartum Haemorrhage

**Author:**
World Health Organization (WHO)

**Date Read:** 23/7/26

**Type:**
Clinical Guideline

---

### Summary

The guideline provides evidence-based recommendations for preventing and treating postpartum haemorrhage. It emphasizes early recognition, rapid intervention, and the use of proven treatments such as oxytocin and tranexamic acid.

---

### Key Findings

- Oxytocin is recommended for prevention.
- Tranexamic acid should be administered early when indicated.
- Early recognition significantly improves patient outcomes.
- Prompt intervention reduces maternal mortality.

---

### Questions I Have

- Which warning signs should be included in Project Asteria?
- Can AI identify patients at risk before complications occur?

---

### How This Helps Project Asteria

- Develop early warning alerts.
- Improve maternal risk assessment.
- Support healthcare workers with faster screening.

---

### Ideas Generated

- Postpartum haemorrhage screening.
- Emergency warning system.
- AI-assisted clinical decision support.
